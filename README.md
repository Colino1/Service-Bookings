# Bookline

Bookings, reminders and lost-money tracking for any small business: cleaners, tailors, tutors, mechanics, photographers, barbers, repair shops and more.

Most small businesses take bookings in WhatsApp messages and lose money to no-shows and to customers who quietly stop coming. Bookline puts every booking in one diary, makes reminders one tap, and shows the owner what they are losing.

## Status
Bookline is in its **testing phase**. The files here are an early version built to learn from real businesses. The full app, with a customer booking page showing free times and automatic reminders, will be built when funding is available.

## How it works
1. A customer taps the business's booking link and starts a WhatsApp chat. The message is already written: service, day, time and name.
2. The owner agrees a time and taps **New booking** in Bookline. The service is picked from a dropdown, and a service that is not listed can be added on the spot.
3. Before the booking, the owner taps **WhatsApp reminder**. The message is already written.
4. Afterwards the owner taps **Came** or **No-show**. Bookline adds up what was earned and what was lost.

## What is inside
- **Bookings:** one diary by day, with the next booking highlighted and a "Did they come?" check-in
- **Money board:** earnings for the last 7 days and money lost to no-shows over 30 days
- **Customers:** an automatic customer list, and a "bring them back" list for people who stopped coming, with a ready message for each
- **Grow:** tomorrow's reminders in one list, quiet-day posts to fill empty days, a 30-day business report, and thank-you messages that ask for referrals
- **Calculator:** shows a business owner what no-shows cost them each month, using their own numbers
- **Setup:** business name, country, WhatsApp number, services and prices, reminder message, booking link and backup

Owners set their own prices. Nothing is preset.

## Privacy
Bookline has no server. Business data stays in the browser on the owner's own phone. Use **Copy backup** in Setup now and then, because clearing the browser's data or changing phones removes it.

## Current limits
- Customers book through WhatsApp, and the owner records the booking. Customers cannot yet pick a free time themselves.
- Reminders are one tap each. They are not sent automatically.
- Data lives on one phone.
