Four problems it solves

Customers forget their booking

Reminders open in WhatsApp with the message already written. One tap to send. The Grow tab lists tomorrow’s bookings so you can send them all in about a minute.

Bookings get lost in chats

Every booking goes into one diary by day. The next booking is always highlighted, and you are warned if two bookings share a time.

Customers quietly stop coming

Bookline shows who has not visited in three weeks and gives you a ready message for each one.

You cannot see where the money goes

The money board shows what you earned this week and what you lost to no-shows. A monthly report shows your best service, busiest day and repeat customers.

How to use it

  1. Set up in two minutes

    Open the app, go to Setup, and add your business name, country, WhatsApp number and services. You decide every price.

  2. Share your booking link

    Copy your link from Setup and put it in your Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp bio. Customers tap it and message you to book, with a form already typed.

  3. Add each booking and send a reminder

    Tap New booking, pick the service from the dropdown, and fill in the day and time. Before the booking, tap WhatsApp reminder.

  4. Tap Came or No-show

    After the booking, tell Bookline what happened. Your money board and reports fill in by themselves.

Tip: in your phone browser menu, choose Add to Home screen, and Bookline opens like an app.

Questions

Is this the finished app?

No. It is an early testing version. The full app will be built when funding is available, and what testers tell us decides what comes first.

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. They tap your booking link and message you on WhatsApp.

What if I change my phone?

Open Setup, tap Copy backup, and paste it into Restore backup on the new phone.

Who can see my customers’ details?

Only you. They stay in the browser on your phone. Bookline has no server.

How do I pay?

Tap Pay on WhatsApp in the app and send your payment. You receive an access code. Paste it in the app and tap Unlock.

Can I try it before I set up my own business?

Yes. Open the demo, pick a type of business, and look around with example data.
